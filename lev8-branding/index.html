THE BRAND CHALLENGE

Give an ambitious AI product a recognizable identity — and an approachable personality that people could connect with.

I shaped Lev8’s identity and website visuals, extended the brand into event materials, and worked with external illustrators and our operations team to carry it into new content.

01 / BRAND ORIGIN

A new name.
A bigger possibility.

As Lumo became Lev8, we wanted the name to express “leveraging infinite opportunities.” The identity brought that idea into a simple, recognizable mark.

THE IDEALeverage

A small force. A greater possibility.

+
THE SYMBOL8 → ∞

Opportunity without a fixed limit.

THE NAMELev8

The ambition behind the brand.

DESIGN DECISION

I combined the lever and the form of an 8 into the logo, giving the name a visual expression that could extend into the rest of the identity.

02 / BRAND PERSONALITY

An AI teammate,
with a little character.

I created the original little-eyed character from half of the Lev8 logo. Its simple black silhouette and expressive eyes gave the AI product a warmer, more approachable presence.

This core character became the foundation for the later visual extensions developed with an external designer.

Original character design by me.

03 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

One language.
Many ways to show up.

Dark shapes, white space and an amber accent form the foundation. The mascot and supporting illustrations add warmth across product screens and marketing materials.

Grounded

Dark typography and simple forms give the identity a clear, confident base.

Warm

Amber accents and expressive characters make the AI product feel more approachable.

Adaptable

A shared visual vocabulary connects small icons, illustrations and larger compositions.

REUSABLE CONTENT TEMPLATES

A recognizable frame
for different stories.

I designed a set of article banner templates using the brand’s palette, typography and geometric forms. Different layouts give market insights, growth articles and playbooks their own expression within the same identity.

Article banner templates designed by me.

06 / COLLABORATION & HANDOFF

A brand the team
could keep building.

With limited time, I combined hands-on design with external collaboration. After establishing the branding, I also delivered a set of reusable AIGC prompts to our operations team for ongoing content creation.

01 / EXTERNAL COLLABORATION

One original character.
A wider visual vocabulary.

I established the little-eyed core character, then worked with an external visual designer to extend it into additional forms, expressions and supporting icons. I communicated the brand direction and coordinated the collaboration.

My contribution: original character design, brand direction and design coordination.
External designer: character extensions, icons and supporting illustrations.

Make the identity usable by others.

I turned the established visual direction into an AIGC prompt set, enabling our operations colleagues to generate new branded content in batches.
